# Superstore Sales Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Superstore sales, profitability, shipping costs, and regional performance. The report explores business performance across product categories, sub-categories, states, cities, and customer segments.

The dashboard is organized into two pages: **Category Analysis** and **Region Analysis**, allowing users to explore different aspects of sales and operational performance.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze sales and profit across product categories and sub-categories.
- Examine monthly sales trends.
- Compare sales performance across states.
- Evaluate profitability by state and product category.
- Explore sales distribution across cities and customer segments.
- Analyze shipping costs by state and order priority.
- Examine product counts and profitability across states.
- Explore shipping modes using an interactive slicer.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Category Analysis

This page focuses on product and sales performance, including:

- Sales by state.
- Sales distribution by sub-category.
- Monthly sales trends.
- Profit distribution by state and category.
- Key performance indicators: Total Sales and Total Quantity.

### 2. Region Analysis

This page focuses on geographical and operational performance, including:

- Shipping costs by state and order priority.
- Product count and profit by state.
- Profit by state.
- Sales by city and customer segment.
- Interactive filtering by shipping mode.
- Key performance indicator: Total Profit.
